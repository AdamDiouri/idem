# Slice 2.5 — Relay and fake shop

*Merged 2026-11-11 · [PR #17](https://github.com/AdamDiouri/idem/pull/17) · [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-11-slice-2.5-relay-and-fake-shop.md)*

Every workflow outcome now reaches its shop. A new process, the relay, delivers each outbox event to the shop's webhook URL, signed, at least once; a fake shop that misbehaves on purpose proves in every chaos run that the shop ends up knowing exactly what idem decided.

## What shipped

- **`idem relay`** (any number may run). It claims due events under a lease, `POST`s the frozen payload with `Idem-Event-Id`, `Idem-Event-Type` and `Idem-Signature` (the inbound HMAC scheme, with the shop's secret), and records `DELIVERED`, a retry with backoff, `DEAD` after 15 attempts (about 16 hours on average), or `NO_ENDPOINT` for a client without a URL (D19). It never follows a redirect and reads at most 4 KiB of an answer.
- **Fencing for relays.** The outbox gained `lease_epoch` (migration 00016): a relay that paused past its lease cannot overwrite the result of the relay that took over (D7).
- **Configuration from the environment only:** `IDEM_CLIENT_WEBHOOKS` and `IDEM_CLIENT_WEBHOOK_SECRETS`. A URL without a secret, a bad URL or a short secret stops the relay at startup, naming the client.
- **Operators:** `idem admin outbox list` and `redeliver` (audited, refused if the relay still has the event or the operator's view is stale); alerts `IdemOutboxBacklog` and `IdemOutboxDead`, each with a runbook section.
- **Old outcomes are not announced.** Events backfilled at the 2.4 upgrade are `SKIPPED`: the first relay would otherwise have pushed every historical outcome to shops at once. An operator can still send one.
- **The fake shop** verifies signatures, processes each event ID once, fails on request (`down`, `slow`, `5xx`, `ack_then_5xx`, `ack_after_timeout`) and reports what it learned. It runs in compose as the demo client's shop, and in chaos.
- **Invariant I9**, and I4 now also requires an empty outbox once a run settles.

## What broke or surprised us

- **The first draft of the I4 change failed two older tests.** They finished workflows and expected a settled system, but their events were still `PENDING`. That was I4 being right: a system that has not told the shop is not settled. The tests now mark the events delivered, as a relay would.
- **A false lint alarm, and a slip fixing it.** The linter could not see that a test helper closed the response body; returning just the status code fixed it. A blanket rename then broke the helper's own return line, caught at once by `go vet`.
- **One slow shop would have delayed every other shop (final review, Important).** Each relay delivered one event at a time, so a shop hanging to the 10s timeout queued everyone else's outcomes behind its own. Each relay now runs `IDEM_RELAY_CONCURRENCY` deliveries (default 4), at most `IDEM_RELAY_PER_CLIENT` (2) for one client. The first version of the cap was soft (parallel claims all read "0 busy" and the test caught three slots taken by the stuck shop); claims now take a transaction-level advisory lock, so the count is exact.
- **A typo in a client ID would have silently stopped its deliveries (final review, Important).** `IDEM_CLIENT_WEBHOOKS="a=…, b=…"` created a client `" b"`; the real `b`'s events became `NO_ENDPOINT` with no alert. The lists are now parsed by hand: trimmed, IDs without spaces, errors that never quote a secret. The relay warns at startup about a configured client with no workflows, and `idem admin outbox redeliver-all` recovers a client's events in one command.
- **Smaller review fixes:** `redeliver` guards on the event's version (its lease epoch, which only grows) instead of its attempts, which return to the same number after a redelivery; `list --all` shows delivered events; an error text cut at 500 bytes no longer splits a character (Postgres would have refused it on every attempt); a failed write is counted as `error`, not under an empty label; chaos refuses `--relays 0`; and a test now has a second relay really take an event over.
- **Parking varied more than before.** Seed 1 parked 11 workflows (earlier slices: 2 to 4). All were `permanent_after_in_doubt`, the pivot rule, which the verdict accepts; the rebalanced fault mix explains it.

## Evidence

- Chaos campaigns, seeds 1, 2 and 3 (90s): PASSED, 0 violations. The shop learned 873, 897 and 886 outcomes, every event `DELIVERED`, with 137, 100 and 99 redeliveries recognised as duplicates; every shop fault fired, and both relay failpoints, `kill_relay` and `kill_all_relays` happened while work was in flight.
- Relay tests against a real database: signed delivery, retry with backoff (F31), `DEAD` after the budget against a refused connection, a late `2xx` treated as a failure, a redirect not followed, at most 4 KiB read, a lost lease fenced, and a crash after the shop's answer redelivering the same event (F32).
- Compose smoke: a payout's outcome reached the fake shop in 4 seconds; with the shop answering `500`, the event waited `PENDING` with `shop answered 500` and was delivered on the next retry after the fault was cleared; `redeliver` sent a delivered event again and the shop counted a duplicate; `idem check` 0 violations.
- `make test` (17 packages), `make lint`, `make vuln` (0 vulnerabilities), `make build-release`, 16 alert rules.
- After the review fixes (parallel delivery with a per-shop cap), the three campaigns passed again with 0 violations: the shop learned 878, 894 and 883 outcomes, all delivered, with 108, 62 and 117 redeliveries deduplicated. One seed-3 run failed a vacuity rule because both of its `pause_worker` events found their target worker mid-restart; it passed on rerun (a harness flake, noted for later).
