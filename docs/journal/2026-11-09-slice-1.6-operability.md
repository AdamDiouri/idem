# Slice 1.6 — Operability

*Merged 2026-11-09 · [PR #5](https://github.com/AdamDiouri/idem/pull/5) · [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-09-slice-1.6-operability.md) · written retrospectively on 2026-11-10 from the PR and commit record*

When idem cannot finish a workflow without guessing about money, it parks it in `NEEDS_ATTENTION`. This slice gives the on-call engineer a way to see it, understand it and resolve it without hand-written SQL. It completes milestone 1.

## What shipped

- **Audited admin actions** (`idem admin`): `list`, `retry-step`, `confirm-step --provider-ref`, `compensate`, `fail`, and `new-generation --confirm-no-effect` (re-send under a new key once "no effect" is verified). Each is one transaction that locks the parked step and its workflow, reuses the steps' own hooks, bumps the fencing token when handing work back, and writes an `admin_actions` audit row.
- **Guards.** Irreversible actions need `--confirm-no-effect` whenever money may have moved. The refund's charge reference is now immutable by trigger, and provider references are unique by index.
- **Prometheus metrics** on every process (step outcomes including fenced commits, provider latency, state gauges from the sweeper). Labels never carry IDs.
- **Prometheus in compose with 8 alert rules,** each linking to the runbook, including two that watch the sweeper itself.
- **A runbook** (`docs/runbooks/needs-attention.md`): for every parking reason, whether money may have moved, how to investigate and which command to run.
- The chaos report now shows fenced commits and in-doubt resolutions.

## What broke or surprised us

- **`compensate` could pay both ways.** With only `--reason`, compensating a payout that had actually paid both paid the merchant and refunded the customer. Reproduced by the reviewer; `--confirm-no-effect` is now required.
- **Doubt was judged only from `dead_reason`.** A refund refused after an ambiguous attempt lost its doubt on retry. Doubt is now also read from the attempt history.
- **A refused refund had no way to close,** and `confirm-step` trusted any reference. It now accepts the reference of a refund made outside idem, and refuses references already recorded on another step.
- **Alerts lied in two ways.** The over-limit gauge never cleared after a crash on a final attempt. And when the sweeper died, every state alert resolved silently. Fixed, with sweeper-down and stale-snapshot alerts.
- **`retry-step` as planned would have blocked a step forever** by reusing attempt numbers. It now keeps the history and extends the budget.
- **Stale decisions and a crash loop.** Admin commands now require `--expect-version`, checked under the row lock, so an operator acting on an old listing is refused. The stuck alert first paged on old workflows (10,800s after a retry); counting every claim as progress then hid crash loops (0.045s idle). Progress is now a status change or a finished, non-abandoned attempt.
- Smaller: the metrics port moved off node_exporter's `:9100` to `:8090`; exit codes are decided by error type, which fixed `idem admin bogus` exiting 0; a flaky chaos test was simply under-loaded and rightly judged vacuous.

## Evidence

- Every admin action raced against the others, 10 rounds each, with workers running: exactly one wins, losers leave no audit row, step or posting. By mutation, both race tests fail without the row lock.
- Compose smoke test: a parked payout appeared in `admin list`, two alerts fired, `compensate --confirm-no-effect` refunded with an audit row, a second `compensate` was refused, `idem check` 0 violations, and the alerts cleared.
- Chaos: a 60s campaign with 110 in-doubt steps resolved; after the follow-up fixes, seed 753284 with 1,000 submissions, 115 failpoint crashes, 0 violations.
- `make test`, `make lint` 0 issues, `make build-release`, `promtool check config` (8 rules).

## Left open

- Sweeper snapshot cost (milestone 3) and `/metrics` access control (milestone 4), recorded in the design doc.
- Minors: `idem help bogus` exits 0; `idem migrate down` runs migrations up; a blank `--reason` exits 1, not 2; the race tests do not exercise the version check; same-reference `confirm-step` on two workflows returns a raw unique-violation error.
