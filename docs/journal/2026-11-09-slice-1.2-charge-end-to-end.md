# Slice 1.2 — Charge step end to end

*Merged 2026-11-09 · [PR #1](https://github.com/AdamDiouri/idem/pull/1) · [plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-09-slice-1.2-charge-step-end-to-end.md) · written retrospectively on 2026-11-10 from the PR and commit record*

This slice makes the `charge` step actually run: a worker claims it, calls a payment provider and records the result. Crashes, pauses and ambiguous answers at any point must never charge a customer twice.

## What shipped

- **Leases and fencing.** Workers claim steps with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` under a lease. Completion is a compare-and-swap on `(id, lease_epoch, status='RUNNING')`. `lease_epoch` is the fencing token: a worker that lost its lease writes nothing.
- **Lease-expiry reclaim.** A sweeper moves steps whose lease expired (a crashed or paused worker) to `IN_DOUBT` and bumps the fencing token. The design doc records this as pulled forward from 1.3.
- **Ambiguity is not failure.** Every provider call sends the step's stored idempotency key. A timeout, `5xx`, `409`, `429`, or a `2xx` with a wrong amount or unreadable body makes the step `IN_DOUBT` ("money may have moved"), never "failed".
- **A double-entry ledger,** written in the same transaction as the step's success. The database enforces it with a deferred balance trigger, append-only triggers and `UNIQUE(step_id, kind)`.
- **A fake payment provider** that behaves like Stripe's idempotency keys (replay, `409` while in flight, `422` on reuse). It injects faults before or after the money moves, and its effects oracle is the ground truth for checks.
- **Invariant checker v0** (`idem check`): I1, at most one charge per workflow, and I2, the ledger balances. Plus in-process failpoints at every step boundary, which compile out with `-tags nofailpoint`.

## What broke or surprised us

- **A panic in a completion hook killed the worker process.** An independent review of the branch found it. The panic is now recovered and the step takes the lease path. A test failed first.
- **Typos in fault modes or failpoint names were silently ignored.** The same review found it; unknown names are now rejected, again with a test that failed first.
- The reviewer found no interleaving of crashes, pauses, sweeps and retries that produced a second charge, a second ledger posting or a second payout step.

## Evidence

- Each engine failure test ends with a zero-violation invariant check:
  - F4–F6 and crashes after claim and after commit: `TestWorker_CrashRecovery_F4_F5_F6`
  - F7–F9 ambiguous outcomes: `TestWorker_AmbiguousRetriesWithSameKey_F7_F9`
  - F10 decline, F12 paused worker fenced, F13 late provider call deduplicated
  - F25 panics in a step or hook; F27 a duplicate charge is detected
- `make test` (11 packages, `-race`, real Postgres 18), `make lint` 0 issues, `make build-release` with failpoints compiled out.
- Compose smoke test: one workflow charged once, `idem check` 0 violations. With `timeout_after_effect` injected live, attempt 1 went `IN_DOUBT` and attempt 2 succeeded by replay, still one charge per workflow.

## Left open

- Review minors deferred, most to 1.3: a shutdown grace bound, an index for the sweeper, a currency check on success, and test-hygiene items.
- Retries still use a fixed delay; backoff, dead letters and key retention come in 1.3.
